Building Safer Salesforce AI Automation

Enterprise automation should optimize for controlled outcomes, not simply for how quickly an AI model can call an API.

Understand permissions first

Read the user's role, available capabilities and object-level constraints before proposing an action. Authorization should be enforced by application logic and Salesforce permissions.

Use a proposal step

Let the AI explain the intended change before execution. A proposal gives deterministic validators and, when required, a human reviewer a clear object to approve.

Validate the payload

Validate field values, required relationships, allowed operations and deployment constraints outside the model. Treat model-generated parameters as untrusted input.

Require approval for risk

High-impact operations can require role-based approval while low-risk reads remain automated. This keeps the workflow fast without removing governance.

Audit every decision

Record the request, actor, proposed operation, validation result, approval state and execution outcome. Auditability turns an AI assistant into an operational system that can be reviewed and improved.
